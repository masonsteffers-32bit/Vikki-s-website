About Me

Victoria Steffers

I have been telling stories through a lens since I got my first camera at 11. What started as a YouTube channel I ran as a kid grew into a real passion for media, and I’ve been sharpening my editing and production skills ever since.

At Tarleton State University, I shot photography and video at athletic events and contributed content to the university yearbook through 2025. I then moved to Colorado to continue my studies at the University of Colorado Colorado Springs, where I joined the UCCS Marketing and Social Media team in March 2026. There, I design content and help manage the university’s social media accounts.

I’m drawn to work that’s creative, has humor, and built on real connection with an audience. Whether it’s a game day highlight or a scroll stopping post, my goal is the same. I tell a story people want to engage with.
